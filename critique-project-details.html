Project page review · Oct 2026

Your case studies look premium, but visitors won’t remember them.

Score: 20 out of 32 — okay, not great yet. The dark cinematic style works. The story doesn’t stick because the same image repeats, every chapter looks the same, and the ending goes nowhere.

20/32

Think of it like a film trailer that shows the same shot four times. People feel something is off, even if they can’t name it.

What’s working — keep this

  • Sticky chapter menu. Problem → Idea → Result stays visible while scrolling. Good for reading.
  • Dark theme is consistent. No random light sections, no clashing colors.
  • Numbers + quote at the end. “+34%” and “under 30 seconds” are exactly what clients remember. They’re just too quiet right now.

Fix first — in plain order

1. Stop repeating the same image.
On the Zizi page the exact same picture appears 4 times. A visitor thinks: “Is this real work?”
Do this: use 1 wide opening shot, then close-ups (verification badge, payment step, finished state). Delete one of the two identical galleries.

2. Make the top tell me why I should care.
Right now the top just says “Zizi” with the same sentence as the list page. No year, no role, no result.
Do this: bigger title, one line under it with Year / Your role / Result — e.g. “2025 · Design + Build · +34% verified leads”.

3. Make every chapter look different.
Problem, Idea and Result all use the same cards and same gaps, so readers tune out.
Do this: Problem = text + image side by side. Idea = full-width images. Result = huge numbers. Same menu, different scenes.

4. Fix the ending.
After a long scroll there’s a huge empty gap, then it just links to the other project — forever looping between 2 projects, no way to contact you.
Do this: halve the gap, show a thumbnail, add two links: “Back to all work” and “Start your project → Contact”.

5. Small readability fixes.
Grey labels are too faint, menu links are hard to tap on phones, bullet points look default.
Do this: brighten labels, make menu taps 44px tall, style bullets like the rest of the site.

How visitors feel now

  • First 5 seconds: “Nice and dark, looks expensive” — then “wait, why just the word Zizi?”
  • Middle: long scrolling, same cards again, attention drops.
  • End: “That’s it? Just the other project?” No urge to contact.

Technical check — one line

Automated scan found 1 tiny note (a 32px icon flagged as text — ignore it). No broken code. Headings, alt text and navigation structure are fine. Anchors need an offset so titles don’t hide under the sticky menu.
